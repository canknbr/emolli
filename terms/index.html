1. What Emolli is

Emolli is a personal diary for eczema and related skin conditions. It records what you enter, shows patterns in your own data and produces reports you can hand to a clinician. The free tier covers your daily diary, its history and data export, with limits on stored photos and medication reminders that the app shows wherever they apply; the Pro subscription removes those limits and adds the full analysis, the doctor report and cloud backup.
