Support

Answers to the questions we get most. Anything else: cankanbur1@gmail.com. We reply within two working days.

What is free and what is Pro?

Free: daily check-ins and their full history, food, sleep, stress and product logs, medication records, dose logging and adherence, treatment plan tracking and data export, plus two progress photos at a time and reminders for one medication. Viewing and comparing the photos you keep is free. Pro is the reading on top of your own diary: likely triggers with the evidence behind each one, the daily flare-risk forecast, weekly and monthly summaries, guided elimination trials and the doctor-ready PDF report, plus cloud backup, unlimited photos and reminders for every medication. Reaching a free limit never deletes anything you already have.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple. Open the Settings app › your name › Subscriptions › Emolli, or open Emolli › Profile › Manage subscription. Cancelling at least 24 hours before the current period ends stops the next charge; the free trial converts to a paid plan unless cancelled before it ends.

I paid but Pro is locked. How do I restore?

Open the Pro screen in Emolli and tap Restore purchases. Make sure the device is signed in with the same Apple Account that bought the subscription.

How do I delete my account and data?

Emolli › Profile › Settings (gear) › Data & Privacy › Delete Account. This removes your account, every record and every photo from our servers immediately. Delete All Data on the same screen removes every record and photo from this device and from our servers but keeps the account.

Can I export my data?

Yes, at any time and free: Profile › Settings (gear) › Data & Privacy › Export Data. You get your records as one JSON file (photos stay in the app; save them one by one from the photo gallery), plus the doctor PDF with Pro.

Is Emolli medical advice?

No. Emolli is a diary and a pattern finder, not a medical device. It shows statistical observations from your own logs; it does not diagnose, treat or replace a dermatologist. Take the report to your doctor rather than acting on it alone.

Does Emolli work offline?

Yes. Everything is stored on your phone first. Cloud backup is part of Emolli Pro and needs an account; your data then syncs to servers in the European Union.

Which languages are supported?

The app is available in English, Turkish, German and Spanish. The App Store listing exists in more languages, but the interface uses one of those four.
