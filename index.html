Emolli: Eczema & Skin Tracker

Flare Triggers & Doctor Report

Eczema flares are not random. Emolli is the skin diary that looks for what comes before yours: the foods, weather, sleep, stress and products that keep showing up before a bad week. It turns months of check-ins into a report your dermatologist can read in a minute.

Log a flare in seconds, photograph your skin, and let the analysis do the remembering. Daily check-ins, your full history and data export are free, with two progress photos at a time and reminders for one medication. Pro adds the answers on top: likely triggers with the evidence behind each one, a daily flare-risk forecast, weekly and monthly summaries, guided elimination trials, the doctor-ready PDF, unlimited photos and reminders for every medication. Try Pro free for 3 days on the yearly or monthly plan.
